## Hand check (added after the run)

Two degenerate patterns the automatic checks above missed. These checks are now in `bakeoff.ts`.

| Model | Plan is just the first options in listed order | Reasons only echo the option label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `llama3.2:1b` | 30/30 | 20/30 |
| `qwen3.5:0.8b` | 21/30 | 5/30 |
| `gemma3:1b` | 30/30 | 18/30 |

- The options are listed nearest first, so "c0, c1, c2" means "do the three nearest things". That explains llama3.2's and gemma3's high top-3 agreement with the reference.
- Only qwen3.5:0.8b actually chooses and explains. For example, "Resting at the dock cools me down and fills my battery, which is crucial when signal is very low" is grounded in its beliefs and state.
- **Verdict:** qwen3.5:0.8b for `plan`. It is 6 s slower at p50, but it's the only one that plans.
